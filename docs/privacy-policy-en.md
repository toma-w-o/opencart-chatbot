## AI chat assistant

Our website has a chat assistant that answers questions about products, delivery and orders. The assistant is an artificial intelligence (AI) program, not a person. If you wish, the conversation can be handed over to a member of our staff.

**Controller:** [ЮРИДИЧЕСКО ЛИЦЕ], company number [ЕИК], address: [АДРЕС]. For questions and requests about your personal data: [ИМЕЙЛ ЗА ЛИЧНИ ДАННИ].

**What we process.** The content of the messages you write; the details you give us in the conversation yourself (for example name, e-mail, phone, address, order number); photos, if you attach any; the page you are writing from; your IP address and browser details. If you are logged in, the chat uses your account to show your orders. If you check an order without an account, we ask for the order number and the phone number used for it.

Please do not send card numbers, IBANs, national ID numbers, passwords or health information in the chat. If you do, the system masks them automatically before storing anything.

**Why, and on what basis.** To answer your questions and handle requests about orders, delivery, returns or stock – steps taken at your request before entering into, or to perform, a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). To protect the website against abuse and to improve the quality of the answers – our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Who receives the data.**
- Anthropic PBC (USA) – provider of the AI model that writes the answers. It receives the content of the conversation and the data needed for the answer. For order questions the model receives only the city and the street without the house number, and no phone or e-mail. Data is transferred under a data processing agreement with [standard contractual clauses / a European Commission decision – specify].
- Telegram – when the conversation is handed over to staff, it is shown to our staff in a private Telegram group. Topics are deleted [7] days after the conversation ends.
- Cloudflare, Inc. – the Turnstile service that protects against automated requests; when a check runs it receives your IP address and technical browser data.
- Our hosting provider and mail server – to store the data and send e-mails.

We do not sell the data or use it for advertising. We make no automated decisions with legal effects for you.

**How long we keep it.** Conversations – up to [6] months; IP address and browser details – up to [30] days; photos – up to [30] days; requests (call-back, stock alert, return, order change) – up to [12] months. Conversations marked for quality improvement are kept beyond that only in anonymised form. Deleted data may remain in server backups for up to [backup period].

**In your browser** the chat stores only technical data (conversation identifier, message draft, whether the chat is open, sound setting). It does not use tracking cookies.

**Your rights.** You have the right to access your data, to receive a copy, to ask for correction, erasure or restriction of processing, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can delete the conversation yourself from the "⋯" menu in the chat. For anything else write to [ИМЕЙЛ ЗА ЛИЧНИ ДАННИ] – we reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Bulgaria: the Commission for Personal Data Protection, www.cpdp.bg; in Greece: the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, www.dpa.gr).
